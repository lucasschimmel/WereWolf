# Game implementation

The game uses a single Convex deployment for room membership, server-authoritative rules and realtime projections. Each seat has a browser-generated private credential. Invite codes identify rooms and cannot authorize existing seats. The host seat is assigned at creation and stays fixed across disconnects and rematches.

The frontend keeps lobby and match projections separate from scene animation. Avatar movement is bounded and interpolated, and hidden during nighttime play.

Movement is ephemeral and bypasses React state. When `VITE_RELAY_URL` is set, each client asks Convex for a five-minute HMAC seat ticket (`game.ticket`, signed with the `RELAY_SECRET` env var shared with the relay) and joins its room's Durable Object in `apps/relay` over WebSocket. Clients send stamped poses at 15 Hz. The relay validates bounds, rate-limits each socket, fans poses out to the other sockets, and keeps the latest pose per socket in its hibernation attachment, so late joiners receive a snapshot. Nothing is persisted. Without a relay, or after four failed connection attempts, clients fall back to `game.move` / `game.positions` at 5 Hz. Receivers buffer snapshots per player (`shared/motion.ts`), map sender clocks with a per-sender minimum-transit offset, and render remote players 120 ms in the past (260 ms on the fallback), extrapolating at most 150 ms. The relay does not check phase or liveness: Convex stays the authority, and renderers ignore positions outside free phases and for dead players. Shared rules define official cards, legal decks, night turns and phase deadlines. The server applies one scheduled transition per phase revision, and evaluates victory after all death effects.

## Night turns

A night is a sequence of turns derived from the dealt deck (`nightTurns` in `shared/rules.ts`): pack, seer, guard, then Workshop cards, followed by the witch (`potions` phase) when that card was dealt. `matches.turn` holds the current turn and is public. When the room reveals roles on death (`revealsOnDeath`: every mode except Mystery and Workshop without reveal), the turn of a dead holder is skipped. In hidden modes the slot is kept but lasts a random masked 4–8 s window picked server-side when it starts (`maskedSeconds`), and a live holder's turn never ends before a 4 s floor, so timing cannot single out a role. The hunter's `nightShot` phase only runs when a hunter died during the night (`matches.hunter` set), exactly like `dayShot`. A turn ends early once its actors are done (`isTurnComplete` in `convex/engine.ts`): the seer chose (2 s grace), every living wolf has a ballot or abstention, the guard or a custom card chose or passed, the witch pressed "Valider" (`choose` action `done`), the hunter shot or passed, and, for `vote` and `runoff`, every living voter has a ballot (abstaining counts). After a 3 s grace `settleEarly` (`convex/phases.ts`) moves `matches.end`, bumps `revision` and schedules a new `advance`; the old scheduled call becomes a no-op, and any later change in the slot restarts the grace. Debate, intro and dawn never end early, and paused matches are untouched. Only the current turn's holders can act (`turnAbility`). The seer's vision is revealed as soon as she chooses and cannot be changed. Protection and the pack's attack are resolved when the last action turn ends, before the witch's turn.

## Interface

Players land on a private village square before joining a room: a left panel sets their name and look (kept in the browser), a right panel creates or joins a room and sends both to the server. All targeting happens by pointing at puppets in the 3D scene: hover to aim, click to choose, right click to pass, and Tab / Enter for keyboard play. During a night turn, only the acting player's client moves its camera back onto the square; other players appear there as sleeping silhouettes, and pack members see each other's aims. Votes are public and live: during `vote` and `runoff`, `game.view` exposes `match.ballots` (living voters' non-empty targets, never outside those phases) and every client shows each voter raising an arm toward their target (and turning their head when seated); abstentions show nothing, and ballots remain changeable until the timer ends. Roles are rendered as trading cards (`src/ui/cards.tsx`). If WebGL is unavailable, the scene falls back to plain target buttons. Workshop presets are saved in the host's browser.
